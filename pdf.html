PDF Bench

Seventeen tools built for one job: making PDFs behave.

Whether you're stitching a stack of scanned pages into one file, breaking a report apart, or getting a signed contract down to an email-friendly size the PDF Bench handles it without ever touching a server. Drop a file in, pick your settings, and the finished PDF downloads straight back to your device.
