Why use Mergio for your PDF, image, video, and audio files?

Most online file converters ask you to upload your PDF, photo, video, or audio file to a remote server, wait in a queue, then download the result | often behind a paywall or a daily limit. Mergio works differently. Every tool on this site, from merging PDFs to compressing a video or converting an audio track, runs entirely inside your own browser tab using your device's processing power. Your file is never transmitted anywhere, which means there's no upload wait, no privacy risk, and no server-side storage of your documents, photos, or recordings.

The seven benches cover the file types people convert most often. The PDF bench handles merging, splitting, compressing, signing, OCR, and form tools. The Image bench covers background removal, resizing, cropping, and format conversion between JPG, PNG, WebP, and HEIC. The Video bench compresses and converts between MP4, WebM, and MOV, and can trim clips or extract audio. The Audio bench trims, normalizes, and converts between MP3, WAV, and other formats. The Text bench includes OCR, JSON formatting, Markdown conversion, and Base64 encoding. The Converters bench moves files between document, spreadsheet, and image formats such as DOCX, XLSX, and SVG. The Utility bench rounds things out with QR code and barcode generation, password tools, and file hashing.

Because everything runs locally, Mergio has no account system, no upload cap, and no watermark added to your exported files. It's free to use for as many files as you need, whether that's a single PDF merge or a batch of video conversions. If you want to understand how in-browser file processing works under the hood, the MDN File API documentation is a good starting point.

Frequently asked questions

Is Mergio really free to merge, compress, and convert files?

Yes. All 150+ tools across the PDF, image, video, audio, text, converter, and utility benches are free, with no sign-up and no daily limit.

Are my files uploaded to a server?

No. Every PDF, image, video, and audio tool on Mergio processes your file directly in your browser tab. Files never leave your device.

What file formats does Mergio support?

Mergio supports common PDF, JPG, PNG, WebP, HEIC, MP4, WebM, MOV, MP3, WAV, DOCX, XLSX, JSON, and CSV formats, among others, across its seven tool benches.

Do I need to install anything?

No installation is required. Mergio runs entirely in a modern desktop browser. Large video jobs benefit from extra memory, so a desktop browser works best for those.
