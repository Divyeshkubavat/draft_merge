Text Bench

Two tools for getting text back out of an image or a scan.

Got a screenshot, a photo of a whiteboard, or a scanned page and need the actual words? The Text Bench runs real OCR (via Tesseract) to read text out of images, and can pull the existing text layer straight out of a text-based PDF both fully client-side.
