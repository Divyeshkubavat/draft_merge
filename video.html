Video Bench

23 tools that turn your browser into a video workstation.

Compress a clip for sharing, trim it down to the part that matters, pull the soundtrack out as an MP3, or turn a highlight into a looping GIF. The Video Bench runs a real video engine (ffmpeg) entirely inside your browser tab the first tool you open downloads a ~25MB engine once per visit, then every tool after that runs instantly.
