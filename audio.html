Audio Bench

Eight tools for editing, recording, and transforming audio.

Trim a podcast clip, boost the bass, reverse a sound effect, or join multiple tracks into one. The Audio Bench runs a real audio engine (ffmpeg) entirely inside your browser tab the first tool you open downloads a ~25MB engine once per visit, then every tool after that runs instantly.

17 toolsPowered by ffmpeg.wasmFirst run downloads once

No server round-trip

Large audio files never get uploaded anywhere | processing happens on your own device.

Format freedom

Process, filter, trim, or merge tracks of different audio formats instantly.

One-time engine load

The first tool you run in a session fetches the audio engine; every tool after that is instant.
